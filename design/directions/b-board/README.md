# B. Board

- **Idea:** the whole race at once. All four categories sit side by side as columns, so a glance answers "who leads where".
- **Moving around:** a fixed sidebar (search, sections, Watch list count, season switcher). Selecting a % opens the "why" as a popover beside it. Phone: one category at a time under a four-way switch, with a bottom tab bar (Race, Awards, Grid, Watch list, More).
- **Density:** medium-dense. About 5–6 contenders per category visible at once, each in a compact block: % with a bar, precursor line, nomination %, average, marks.
- **Type:** Onest (400–700). Strong weights for titles and %; small, quiet meta lines.
- **Colour:** cool light grey canvas (#F4F4F2) with white panels; Oscar gold for bars, the leader's %, the active section and active marks.
- **Shows:** all four categories, 2025 race as of 20 Dec 2025 (real films, illustrative %), with the "why" open for Best Picture's leader.
