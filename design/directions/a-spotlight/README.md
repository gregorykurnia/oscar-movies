# A. Spotlight

- **Idea:** one category at a time, given room. The % chance to win is the largest thing on the screen, in light-weight numerals, with a thin gold line under each row showing its size.
- **Moving around:** top bar with every section and search (`/`). The four categories are large word tabs under the title; each tab shows its current leader, so the other races are visible without leaving this one. Phone: the tabs scroll sideways; the menu and search sit in the top bar.
- **Density:** airy. Seven contenders fill a desktop screen; precursor results and scores sit on their own lines under the title.
- **Type:** Geist (300–600). Big, tight headings; tabular figures.
- **Colour:** warm white paper (#FCFBF9), ink and stone greys, deep Oscar gold for the leader's %, the bars and the active marks.
- **Shows:** Best Picture, 2025 race as of 20 Dec 2025 (real films, illustrative %), with the "why" open on the leader.
