# A2. Headliner (variant of A)

- **Idea:** the favourite gets a feature block (large title, very large %, its "why" always open, a gold line along the bottom as wide as its chance); the rest of the field follows in a tighter list with small bars.
- **Moving around:** top bar as A; categories switch with a compact pill switch.
- **Density:** one roomy block, then compact rows.
- **Type:** Albert Sans (300–700), bolder headings than A.
- **Colour:** slightly cooler white (#FAFAF8), Oscar gold for the favourite's %, the bars and active marks.
