Oscar Movies: design preview

The visual reference for the build. DESIGN_GUIDE.md is the spec; this file shows it. Every page in the app is assembled from the pieces below and compared against the matching frame here.

Light only. Dark mode isn't designed (design brief). Use the switch above for phone width (390px) and for stress data: very long titles with no spaces, huge counts, missing scores.

v1.1 (2026-10-25) adds the Phase 7 pieces: one flag per film, the year grid's Oscars sort, the strong signals page, and the Watch list's add box, My order with drag handles, and release line with its editor, and an award's all-years page. They are marked v1.1 below. v1.2 adds the Phase 8 pieces: the nomination patterns page, the Oscars year page’s shortlists, and the movie page’s Nomination patterns panel (marked v1.2).

Data is realistic mock data for the design. Percentages and accuracy figures are illustrative, not the app's real output.

Add a film · v1.1

A box at the top of the Watch list that finds any film the app has, by title or original title, accents ignored. 4 · Page templates

One frame per kind of page in the product plan. Each renders inside AppShell (top bar, page, footer) and starts with PageHeader. The app has no forms or settings pages: data changes go through Claude (product plan §3.12), so the only inputs are search, the year switcher, sort and filter controls, the marks, and, on the Watch list (v1.1), the Add a film box, the drag handles and the release editor.

How to pick the five

Documentary, in plain words
  1. Work from the shortlist of 15.
  2. Lock in films with 3+ strong awards, non-US first, and any IDA, European Film Awards or National Society winner.
  3. Next, Spirit Awards nominees, non-US first.
  4. Distrust a film among the five most-nominated with no Spirit or IDA Best Feature nomination, however many critics groups named it.
  5. The last one or two spots are open: prefer a non-US film with a DGA, Cinema Eye or Spirit nomination over a US crowd-pleaser with many critics-group wins.

How to pick the five

International, in plain words
  1. Work from the shortlist of 15.

  3. Next, BAFTA, Golden Globe and Critics Choice nominees: about three in four got in, and most nominees had at least one of them.
