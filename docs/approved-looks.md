# Looks the design approves on purpose; the fresh-eyes checker is told not to report them

- The awards index shows month and year for multi-day festivals and a full winners' date for critics groups, as in the §8.7 reference.
- The awards index shows a coverage pill in place of the date for a partly loaded or missing edition, as in the §8.7 reference.
- Award-year prize links jump to sections in one continuous list. They have no selected tab, as described in guide §8.2.

- Release dates distinguish already out ("Out 3 Oct", omitting the current year) from upcoming ("12 Dec 2026"); guide §8.3.
- Film-row scores end with the compact average and source count ("87 · 4 of 5"). The movie page labels Average and "4 of 5 scores" (guide §7).
- The desktop release editor intentionally floats over later film rows; it does not push those rows down; guide §8.3.
- An open release editor holds an unsaved draft while the row continues to show the saved release note; Save commits and Cancel discards the draft; guide §8.3.
- At 360px the Watch list's six sort choices wrap into three rows; the selected background has a 3px track inset while every label has the same 12px inner padding; guide §8.3.
- Phone release notes stay in the film column beside the mark buttons. A long date/status/platform note may wrap after its inline separator, leaving the platform on the next line and empty space beneath the marks; guide §8.3.
- Desktop film-row numbers and their column headings share a right edge. Stage labels and pills share a left edge, as specified in guide §7.
- Winner rows use a gold wash that fades from the left, as specified in guide §7.
- Watch list gallery filenames beginning with `baseline-` show the row before the new Star control for a layout comparison.
- Geist’s capital T has open space under its crossbar in pairs such as TH, TI and TM. Titles and TMDB use the approved font and keep the original text.
- Phone row crops end at the list edge. The full viewport has 16px gutters, and result groups can wrap on a narrower screen.
- Viewport frames of a scrolled list end wherever the screen ends, so the bottom edge can cut through a film row; the row is whole in its own crop.
- The save-failure toast floats over the rows and section headings beneath it at the bottom of the screen; it can be closed, as guide §7 specifies.
- Race tabs at desktop widths keep the leader on one line and use an ellipsis when its name runs out of room, including on the selected tab (Design Guide §6.3).
- A neighboring phone race tab can fade at the edge beside the chevron that reveals the rest of the scrollable row (Design Guide §6.3).
- In 1× screenshots Geist sets the decimal point close to the digit before it ("3.8" at 12–13px); it reads clearly at 2× and the font is approved (guide §4).
- Desktop race row crops leave out the column headings above the list ("To be nominated", "To win"); the full page shows them, and phone rows say "87% to be nominated" (guide §6.5).
- A film with an International Feature result shows its submitting country's flag, even when the sub line names other production countries (Baghdad Messi: Iraq's flag, "Belgium"); guide §7 Flags.
